Our paper <a href="/publications/hmt/">High Multiplicity Trigger for Long-Lived Particles in CMS Detector</a> is published in <a href="https://doi.org/10.1088/1748-0221/21/07/P07040">JINST</a>. The paper presents a dedicated trigger that targets unusually large hit multiplicities in the CMS muon detectors, a characteristic signature of long-lived particles decaying in the muon system, substantially extending the CMS sensitivity to such signatures during LHC Run 3. I contributed the electron+MDS and muon+MDS cross triggers, which were accepted into the CMS trigger menu in 2023.
