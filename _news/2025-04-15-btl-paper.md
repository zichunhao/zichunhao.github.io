<a href="/publications/btl/">The CMS barrel timing layer, test beam confirmation of module timing performance</a> is available as <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.11209">arXiv:2504.11209</a> and is published in Nuclear Instruments and Methods in Physics Research Section A. The paper demonstrates that the final detector module design achieves the target $30$ to $60~\mathrm{ps}$ time resolution, radiation tolerance, and response uniformity for the High Luminosity LHC. I contributed to the QA/QC procedure, assembled detector modules and trays, and performed thermal cooling studies of the module.
