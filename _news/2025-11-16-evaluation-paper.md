<a href="/publications/foundation-model-evaluation/">An Evaluation of Representation Learning Methods in Particle Physics Foundation Models</a> is available as <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.12829">arXiv:2511.12829</a> and was presented at the ML4PS workshop at NeurIPS 2025. The paper systematically compares contrastive, masked particle modeling, and generative objectives for particle physics foundation models within a unified framework. I served as a mentor on this work, led by Michael Chen.
