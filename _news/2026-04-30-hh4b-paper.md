The CMS analysis I work on, <a href="https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.27044">Improved results on Higgs boson pair production in the 4b final state</a>, is now public and submitted to Physical Review D. It sets the most stringent constraints on $\mathrm{HH}$ production in the 4b final state to date.
