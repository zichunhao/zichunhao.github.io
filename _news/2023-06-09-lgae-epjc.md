My first-author paper <a href="/publications/lgae/">Lorentz Group Equivariant Autoencoders</a> is published in <a href="https://doi.org/10.1140/epjc/s10052-023-11633-5">The European Physical Journal C</a>. The paper develops an autoencoder equivariant to the Lorentz group that outperforms GNN and CNN baselines on unsupervised anomaly detection.
