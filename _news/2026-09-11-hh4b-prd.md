The CMS analysis I work on, <a href="/publications/hig-24-010/">Improved results on Higgs boson pair production in the 4b final state</a>, is published in <a href="https://doi.org/10.1103/w2zp-fckl">Physical Review D</a>. It sets the most stringent constraints on $\mathrm{HH}$ production in the 4b final state to date. I was responsible for the trigger and neural network jet tagger calibrations in this publication.
