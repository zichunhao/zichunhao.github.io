## Abstract

> A search for standard model (SM) nonresonant Higgs boson pair ($\mathrm{HH}$) production is performed in the two bottom quark ($\mathrm{b\bar{b}}$) and all-hadronic two vector boson ($\mathrm{VV \to 4q}$) final states. The search is carried out in proton-proton collisions at 13 TeV with a dataset corresponding to a total luminosity of $138~\mathrm{fb}^{-1}$. The analysis focuses on highly Lorentz-boosted $\mathrm{HH}$ candidates, where each Higgs boson's daughter quarks are all merged inside a single large radius jet. A new global particle transformer (GloParT) classifier is used to effectively perform boosted $\mathrm{VV \to 4q}$ jet identification. The multiplicative modifier of the SM quartic coupling between two Higgs bosons and two vector bosons is observed (expected) to be constrained at 95% confidence level to $\kappa_{2\mathrm{V}} \in [-0.04, 2.05]$ ($[0.05, 1.98]$).
